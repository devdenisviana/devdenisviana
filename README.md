## 👋 Hello! I'm Denis Jerônimo Viana Filho

💻 I am a software and hardware developer specializing in Embedded Systems, Microelectronics, and Mixed-Signal (AMS) Integrated Circuit Design.
My work focuses on low-level systems programming, real-time control, FPGA RTL design, and analog/mixed-signal IC modeling and simulation using the Synopsys EDA suite.

🔍 **Current Focus:**

- **Mixed-Signal IC Design (AMS):** Analog and mixed-signal circuit design and simulation using **Synopsys Custom Compiler**, **PrimeWave Design Environment**, and **PrimeSim** — CMOS 90nm technology.
- **Digital Logic & FPGA:** RTL Design using Verilog, Intel MAX 10 (DE10-Lite), and Quartus Prime.
- **Digital Verification:** Functional simulation and debugging with **Synopsys VCS** and **Verdi**.
- **Embedded Firmware:** Bare-metal and FreeRTOS development for RP2040, ESP32, and STM32.
- **Systems & Infrastructure:** Linux administration (Rocky Linux) and hardware-level network protocols.

📚 **Areas of Expertise:**

- Analog & Mixed-Signal (AMS) Circuit Design: current mirrors, differential pairs, two-stage op-amps, folded cascode amplifiers, and bandgap voltage references
- CMOS Device Characterization: NMOS/PMOS DC behavior, PTAT/CTAT thermal response, ZTC compensation
- Electronic Design Automation (EDA) & IC Schematic/Layout flows
- Field-Programmable Gate Arrays (FPGA) & Hardware Description Languages (HDL)
